## Classification

This change may compose existing owner results into one new, additive authority
contract. It must not reinterpret historical artifacts, call the legacy
transaction, or provide an execution engine. Any raw path or artifact bytes
needed to replay an existing owner are seal-time inputs only and must not enter
the portable bundle.

## Explicit exclusions and blockers

- Historical execution request/authorization `/1` and current read-only `/2`
  retain their bytes, owners and meanings.
- Live approvals `/6`–`/8` are not resource-bound and cannot enter the new
  contract.
- Protected TS maturity `/2` is already documented as not integrated into live
  submission. PR4D does not bridge or weaken that gate.
- The first contract supports only predecessor combinations that the existing
  input/live/resource owners already accept, including current resource-bound
  live approvals `/9`–`/11`.
- Any future adapter must separately prove that its actual immutable stage and
  single submit consume the exact reserved bundle. That proof is outside PR4D.
- No offline result in this task is actual transport, staging, PBS, Gaussian,
  deployment or live compatibility evidence.
