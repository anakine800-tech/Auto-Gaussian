## Deferred gaps and limits

- This PR does not characterize RFC-only Stage A/Stage B transport identity attestation;
  those objects do not exist in v2.5.4 production code and belong to later reviewed PRs.
- Exact live reachability, host-key behavior, RTwin/Windows behavior, PBS dialect behavior,
  Gaussian availability and scheduler side effects remain live-only unknowns. No PR1 test
  may contact them.
- Only the `submit --help` `%nprocshared` `format_help()` `ValueError` is a known defect
  requiring a separate reviewed production change. `submit` action semantics remain in the
  required compatibility projection; updating or skipping that projection is not an acceptable fix.
- Existing broad historical validators remain owned by their current test modules. The full
  offline suite, rather than duplicated PR1 cases, is the compatibility replay for those rows.
