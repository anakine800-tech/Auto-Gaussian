## Exact owner replay

Issuance consumes the reservation capability before replay. Any later failure
is terminal for that reservation authority. Under the existing execution-batch
sibling lock, the package-4 owner rereads and validates:

- the exact `/3` batch, task, attempt, idempotency identity, project, and input;
- the post-reservation ledger revision, resource-state revision/hash, and
  required `submission_uncertain` state;
- the exact tier, cores, memory, walltime, and estimate;
- the policy revision identity `(policy_id, payload_sha256)`;
- the exact gate and its pre-reservation resource-state revision/hash; and
- the same scheduler snapshot bytes, payload identity, and current freshness.

The owner records raw artifact hashes and sizes only as descriptive drift
bindings. Paths, CLI values, portable JSON, Schema validity, hashes, and copied
documents never grant authority.

Consumption repeats the same locked ledger and artifact replay. Any ledger
revision/hash/state change, policy/gate/scheduler byte or semantic change,
tuple or identity splice, scheduler expiry, wall-clock regression, monotonic
clock regression, clock divergence, or capability expiry fails closed. A
failed consumption cannot be retried.

The handle is owner-private, non-copyable, non-serializable, and valid for at
most 30 seconds. Under the registration lock, its private registry binds the
exact capability object identity, an independent owner snapshot of the issued
document, its canonical bytes, the wall-clock issue instant, and a
monotonic-clock instant. Before changing the single-use state, consumption
requires the exact registered class and object and byte-for-byte canonical
equality between the current private slot and the owner snapshot. Replacing and
re-sealing any slot fails closed without consuming the intact registered
capability. The claim reads its capability ID and identity only from the owner
snapshot. Claim issuance separately registers the exact claim object identity,
an owner-owned scope snapshot, and its canonical bytes. Every
`claim.exact_scope()` call requires the exact registered class and object under
the claim-state lock, rejects any private-slot byte drift, and returns a new
deep copy of the owner snapshot rather than reading claim data from the slot.

Issuance and consumption also require the same canonical package-4 module cache
identity; cache replacement and a same-source foreign module fail closed.
Exactly one concurrent caller can consume the intact handle. The resulting
non-copyable claim reports that the replay passed while keeping runner,
transport, qsub, and production-port authority false.
